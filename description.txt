Since update 1.21 the game has its own Coptic culture, called Remenkhemi.
This mod no longer adds a separate culture: it adds Coptic flavor to the game's own.

What it adds:
- Coptic place names in Egypt (Egypt is Kime, Alexandria is Rakote, and more)
- Coptic ruler titles: Pouro (king), Tiouro (queen), Metouro (kingdom)
- Three men-at-arms with their own paintings:
  - Coptic Spearmen
  - Coptic Archers
  - Alexandrian Guard, heavy infantry (needs Household Soldiers)
- Byzantine-style buildings instead of Arab ones
- Custom models for Coptic armies

Who to play:
- In 1066, Kyril, Count of Siwa
- With Silk & Silver, Coptic merchant families in Cairo, Alexandria and Kharga, at every start date
- Or make your own Coptic ruler with the ruler designer
